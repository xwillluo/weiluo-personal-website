Journal Article

Affective Lockdown: Administrative Chaos and Informal Repairing in Urban China during COVID-19

Yan Long, Wei Luo
The China Journal, 93, 51–75, 2025.
Publisher Link (DOI)

Abstract

Targeted lockdowns in urban China depended on more than formal administrative capacity. This study shows how procedural, material, and staffing breakdowns produced administrative disorder and resistance, and how frontline workers used emotional engagement and community relationships to repair the system in practice.

Research Summary

Research Question

How were targeted urban lockdowns implemented when formal administrative arrangements repeatedly broke down?

Main Finding

Frontline workers’ informal affective labor helped secure cooperation and blurred the boundaries among surveillance, service, and confinement. This relational work was essential to sustaining neighborhood governance.

Data and Methods

Fifty in-depth interviews with frontline workers in a southern Chinese city.

Citation
